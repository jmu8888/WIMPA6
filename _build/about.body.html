About WIMPA

From a simple idea to an international competition

The competition is only the form; the platform is the purpose. WIMPA exists so that mobile photographers everywhere can share the stories around them and the feelings behind each image.

The 1st Awards: "Impressions of Washington"

The first Washington Mobile Photography Awards began with a down-to-earth idea: give mobile photography enthusiasts a place to share their work and tell the stories around them. Even the organizers were surprised by the response. Nearly 500 entries arrived, spanning nature, culture, documentary and creative work, and more than 3,000 people watched the online awards ceremony on February 5, 2022.

"Mobile photography may be amateur, but it is anything but low-grade."
Enpu Yang, Chair of the 1st Jury

The 2nd Awards: "Care"

The theme grew from a local impression to a universal one: stories of care between people and nature, between people, and between people and animals. Entries opened to the whole United States and Canada, a seven-member jury was formed, and around twenty photography clubs and community organizations joined as co-organizers.

The 3rd and 4th Awards: going international

The competition became the Washington International Mobile Photography Awards. The 4th edition, themed "Life", drew nearly 2,000 entries from eight countries, judged by nine photographers from China, the United States and Canada. Its awards ceremony in North Bethesda, Maryland welcomed nearly two hundred guests.

The 5th Awards

The 5th WIMPA introduced four themes (Everyday Life, Nature & Landscape, People & Documentary, and Art & Creativity) and the photo series format. Entries were received from November to December 2025, and the winners were celebrated in the Washington, D.C. area in 2026. See the 5th WIMPA winners

The 6th Awards: "Home"

The 6th WIMPA, Washington International Mobile Photography Awards 2026, carries the motto "Mobile Vision. Global Stories." It has two groups and four categories: the theme "Home" (single photo and photo series), and an Open Group for Nature & Landscape and Art & Creativity. Entries are open from November 1 to December 31, 2026, judged by an international jury that now includes Max Rive and Yvette Depaepe of 1x.com. Categories & rules
