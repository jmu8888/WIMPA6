About WMPA

Transform fleeting moments into timeless memories with your phone.

The Washington International Mobile Photography Awards, initiated and hosted by the Washington Cultural Arts Foundation since 2021.

The Greater Washington Mobile Photography Awards (“Awards”) is initiated and hosted by the Washington Cultural Arts Foundation (WCAF). The annual Awards serves as a global platform for mobile photography enthusiasts, both amateurs and professionals, to share, connect, and celebrate the art of capturing life’s most captivating moments through the lens of a mobile device, transcending geographical and cultural boundaries. It’s a place where the beauty of mobile photography intertwines with the compelling stories and diverse emotions behind each photograph.

Established in 2021, the Awards quickly gained international acclaim for its high-caliber entries and a panel of expert judges from the world of photography. Since its inception, the Awards has witnessed double growth, with each edition surpassing the last. The third edition in 2023 saw over 1,000 entries from almost 500 participants, not only from the United States but also from China, Australia, Southeast Asia, and European countries. The remarkable expansion has elevated the competition to an internationally recognized platform, now known as the Washington International Mobile Photography Awards.

Supported by over 30 esteemed photography organizations worldwide, including the Atlantic Photographers Association, the New York Photography Society, the San Francisco Photographer Alliance, and the Canadian International Photography Association, the Awards has become a beacon for mobile photography enthusiasts. Additionally, it has fostered a vibrant community of thousands, actively engaged in sharing their work and participating in expert-led critiques, mobile photography lectures, photography tours, and other enriching activities organized by the Organizing Committee of the Awards.

We extend a warm invitation to mobile photography enthusiasts worldwide to participate in this extraordinary event. Join us in the vibrant capital of the United States, Washington, D.C., for a celebration of creativity, talent, and the art of mobile photography.
