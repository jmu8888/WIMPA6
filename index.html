About the Awards

What matters is the eye behind the lens, not the equipment.

What began in 2021 as a local mobile photography contest in Washington, D.C. has grown into an international competition connecting photographers across North America, Asia, Europe and beyond.

The Washington International Mobile Photography Awards give mobile photographers a platform to capture moments, tell the stories around them, and share the reflections behind each image. Every entry is reviewed anonymously, over multiple rounds, by an independent panel of internationally recognized photographers.
